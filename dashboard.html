Welcome to AuthScope

Let's find the sites you've signed in to with Google, GitHub and your other accounts. It takes about a minute.

  1. Allow AuthScope on the provider sites

    Firefox asks before an extension can use other sites. AuthScope only uses the pages of Google, GitHub and the other providers.

  2. Stay signed in to your accounts

    AuthScope reads the list of linked apps from each account you're signed in to in this browser. Nothing is sent anywhere.

  3. Check all your accounts

    Each account's linked-apps page opens in a background tab, gets read, and closes again.

  4. Pin AuthScope to your toolbar

    Open the Extensions menu (the puzzle-piece icon) and pin AuthScope. Its icon then shows which account you used on the site you're on.
